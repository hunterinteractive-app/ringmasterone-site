Before you import

A little preparation helps you choose the right records and destination. See setup on pages 3–7 of the PDF.

  1. Sign in and review the policies

    Open RingMaster Breeder and sign in with the code sent to your email. Use the same verified email as Show or Club to find your profiles, then review and accept the policies.

  2. Prepare your source

    Keep a current backup and the export your source requires, or have your Global Pedigree login ready. Choose the import options you need. You can skip startup imports and return through Account later.

  3. Choose your destination

    Animal imports need an owned, non-demo herd. Choose an existing herd or add one when your membership allows it. Show history alone and Club profiles do not require creating a herd.

Animals and Show history can be imported separately. If a pedigree program supplies your animals, add Show history if you want entries and results. Select Show animals only when you also need those animal records.

Choose your import source

Each source has its own preparation and review steps. These links open the relevant section of the PDF in a new tab.

Rabbit Register

Export an XML file from Rabbit Register and keep it below 10 MB. Import rabbits, pedigree links, and supported tracking records. Review file notes and possible existing matches before saving.

Rabbit Register instructions · pages 8–11

Kintraks

Export All records as a UTF-8 CSV with comma delimiters and double-quoted text, below 10 MB. Review rabbits and pedigree links, explicitly choose your current herd, and retain original fields in Kintraks history.

Kintraks instructions · pages 12–14

Global Pedigree

Connect with your Global Pedigree login to collect a preview of rabbits, ancestors, and available history. No XML or CSV is needed. Review possible duplicates and the full preview before importing.

Global Pedigree instructions · pages 15–17

RingMaster Show animals

Select your matching Show exhibitor profiles to bring available animal identity details into your chosen herd. No export file is needed. Show entries and results are a separate choice in the same review step.

Show animal instructions · pages 18–19

RingMaster Show history

Import show entries and results from your selected profiles. With animal import off, this creates or changes no animal records. Results that cannot be linked to one animal remain in imported history for review.

Show history instructions · pages 20–21

RingMaster Club profiles

From Account → Import & export → Import RingMaster records, choose Club and select your profiles. This imports exhibitor profiles only; it does not bring in animals or individual Show entries and results.

Club profile instructions · page 22
Startup order: Rabbit Register, Kintraks, Global Pedigree, then RingMaster Show. Unchecked sources are skipped, and both Show options share one review step. Club profile import is available through Account.

After importing

Check your results before putting away your original backup and export.

  • Spot-check familiar animals, parent links, active and historical statuses, and retained records under Account → Imported history.
  • For repeat imports, keep the same destination herd and source name for Rabbit Register or Kintraks, the same account and herd for Global Pedigree, and the same selected profiles for Show or Club.
  • Imports copy information and leave your source records unchanged. They are not live two-way synchronization; existing Breeder details and parent links may remain unchanged on repeat imports.
  • Optional contact setup lets you review your own imported Rabbit Register contact before applying it to your account. See contact setup on page 23.

Need help with a file, profile lookup, or retry? Read troubleshooting on page 24 or email support@ringmasterone.com with the source program, the step that failed, and the visible error.
