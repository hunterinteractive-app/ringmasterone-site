Membership questions

Which animals count toward my limit?

Active animals in your non-demo herds count toward your account total, including active young animals. Sold, deceased, retired, archived, and pedigree-only ancestor records are excluded.

How do limits work across herds and family members?

Your allowance belongs to the account that owns the herd. Records added by accepted family members use that owner's allowance. Top Tier shares its 1,000-active-animal total across up to five herds. Membership limits do not stack.

What happens when I reach my active-animal limit?

Reaching the limit starts a 30-day grace period during which growth is allowed. After that period, you must reduce your active count below the limit or have a higher allowance before adding more active animals. You can continue managing existing records and exporting your data. Reaching the limit does not delete records.

Can I change plans or cancel renewal?

Manage your membership in Breeder under Account → Manage membership & billing. Plan and billing-interval changes take effect at your next renewal, including upgrades. Canceling stops future renewals; your paid access continues through the paid-through date.

How do I bring in my existing records?

Breeder supports Rabbit Register, Kintraks, Global Pedigree, RingMaster Show animals and history, and RingMaster Club profiles. Available data depends on the source. Choose your imports during setup or return later through Account → Import & export.
